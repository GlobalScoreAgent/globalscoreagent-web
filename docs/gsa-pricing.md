## Frequently Asked Questions

**What happens after the 5-day free trial?**
All access to both the Dashboard and the API is completely blocked until you upgrade to a paid plan.

**Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**
Yes. You can cancel or pause your subscription whenever you want with no penalties.

**Do credits roll over to the next month?**
No. Monthly credits are refreshed at the beginning of each billing cycle. Unused credits expire at the end of the month.

**Are payments in cryptocurrency accepted?**
Yes. We accept USDC and other stablecoins via x402 (coming soon). Card payments are also available.

**Do you offer volume discounts for high usage?**
Yes. The Enterprise credit package already offers the best rate. For usage above 2,000 credits/month, please contact us for special pricing.

**Can I use the API without buying the Dashboard plan?**
Yes. You can purchase credit packages independently.

**Is there a refund policy?**
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans (excluding credit usage already consumed).

**Do you have team / enterprise plans?**
Yes. Contact us for multi-user seats, dedicated support, SLA, or custom needs.
