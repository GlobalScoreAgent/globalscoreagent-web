# Index WAMI – Technical Specification

**Version 1.0**  
**Date:** May 19, 2026  
**Index Type:** Wallet Reputation Score (0–100)

---

## Overview

The **Index WAMI** (Wallet Advanced Metrics Index) is a composite reputation score from **0 to 100** that evaluates the overall quality, legitimacy, risk level, and maturity of any on-chain wallet.

It is built from **four independent pillars**, each worth a maximum of **25 points** (total 100 points).  

Each pillar is divided into three sections:
- **Basic** – Foundational quality
- **Intermediate** – Demonstrated capability  
- **Advanced** – Sophisticated maturity

---

## Pillar 1 – Origins & Legitimacy (25 points)

Evaluates how clean and natural the wallet’s funding history is.

### Basic Section (10 points)
- Quality and legitimacy of the very first funds received: **8 points**
- Low risk of mixing services or suspicious fund sources: **2 points**

### Intermediate Section (9 points)
- Absence of heavy reliance on centralized exchange (CEX) inflows: **5 points**
- Healthy diversity of funding sources and senders: **4 points**

### Advanced Section (6 points)
- Very low concentration of inflows and top senders: **3 points**
- Minimal or zero detected mixing risk: **3 points**

---

## Pillar 2 – Portfolio Quality (25 points)

Measures the health, liquidity, and sophistication of the assets held by the wallet.

### Basic Section (10 points)
- Total portfolio value and overall asset health: **7 points**
- High proportion of liquid (easily tradable) assets: **3 points**

### Intermediate Section (9 points)
- Strong diversification across different token types and categories: **6 points**
- Balanced exposure to stable and verified assets: **3 points**

### Advanced Section (6 points)
- Minimal low-quality or high-risk holdings: **3 points**
- Sophisticated portfolio composition (LP positions, verified tokens): **3 points**

---

## Pillar 3 – Activity & Behavior (25 points)

Analyzes whether the wallet’s recent activity looks natural and sustainable.

### Basic Section (10 points)
- Natural volume and balance of inflows vs outflows: **7 points**
- High number of unique, genuine counterparties: **3 points**

### Intermediate Section (9 points)
- Low signs of wash-trading or artificial transaction cycles: **6 points**
- Limited suspicious patterns (shared wallets, timing concentration): **3 points**

### Advanced Section (6 points)
- Very low CEX interaction relative to total activity: **3 points**
- Healthy repeat interaction ratio without red flags: **3 points**

---

## Pillar 4 – Multi-Chain Presence & Maturity (25 points)

Assesses the wallet’s longevity, geographic spread, and consistency across blockchains.

### Basic Section (10 points)
- Long history of consistent activity (age and span): **8 points**
- Active presence across multiple blockchain networks: **2 points**

### Intermediate Section (9 points)
- Balanced and coherent activity patterns across chains: **5 points**
- Overall maturity demonstrated by sustained engagement: **4 points**

### Advanced Section (6 points)
- High number of active chains with consistent behavior: **3 points**
- Strong cross-chain consistency score: **3 points**

---

**Final WAMI Score** = Pillar Origins & Legitimacy (25) + Pillar Portfolio Quality (25) + Pillar Activity & Behavior (25) + Pillar Multi-Chain Presence & Maturity (25)
