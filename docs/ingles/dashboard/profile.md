# Profile

The **Profile** page allows you to manage your account information, Dashboard preferences, and your favorite agents.

## 1. Account

This section displays your general account information:

- **OAuth Account** (Google or GitHub): Your name and avatar are managed automatically by your authentication provider.
- **Display Name**: You can edit the name that will be shown across the platform.
- **Avatar URL**: You can customize the URL of your profile picture.
- **Contact Email** (optional): You can add a contact email address.

---

## 2. Redeem Code

This section allows you to redeem promotional or access codes.

Simply enter the code in the input field and click the **Redeem** button.

---

## 3. Preferences

In this section, you can configure your personal Dashboard preferences:

- **Language**: You can choose between **English** or **Español**.
- **Theme**: You can select between **Dark** or **Light**.

Changes are applied immediately.

---

## 4. Favorite Agents

This section shows the list of agents you have marked as favorites.

Here you can:

- View your list of favorite agents.
- Click on an agent to quickly access its information.
- Remove agents from your favorites list using the **Remove** button.

---

## Usage Tips

- Keep your **Display Name** and **Avatar** updated for better identification across the platform.
- Use the **Favorite Agents** section to have quick access to the agents you use the most.
- Change the **Language** and **Theme** according to your preference for a more comfortable experience.
- The **Redeem Code** section is useful when you receive promotional or special access codes.
