# Subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** page allows you to manage your current plan, view your subscription history, and explore available plans to upgrade.

## 1. Active Subscription

This section displays the information of your current subscription, including:

- Active plan type
- Subscription start date
- End date (if applicable)
- Subscription status

Here you can clearly see which plan you currently have and until when it is valid.

---

## 2. Subscription History

This section shows the complete history of subscriptions associated with your profile.

You can review:

- Plans you have had in the past
- Start and end dates of each subscription
- Plan changes made over time

This is useful for keeping a record of your usage history on the platform.

---

## 3. Available Plans

This section displays the plans available for purchase.

- If you have a **Free** subscription, you can view and subscribe to any of the available paid plans.
- If you already have a paid plan, this section may show upgrade options or additional information based on your current plan.

Here you can compare the different plans and choose the one that best fits your needs.

---

## 4. More Details

This section contains additional information about:

- Payment and billing policies
- How API credits work
- Automatic subscription renewal
- Plan cancellation
- Differences between available plans

It is recommended to review this section if you have questions about how subscriptions or API credits work.

---

## Usage Tips

- Regularly check the **Active Subscription** section to know your plan’s expiration date.
- Review the **Subscription History** if you need to keep track of the plan changes you have made.
- If you have a Free account, visit the **Available Plans** section to evaluate a possible upgrade.
- The **More Details** section is useful for resolving questions about payments, API credits, and service conditions.
