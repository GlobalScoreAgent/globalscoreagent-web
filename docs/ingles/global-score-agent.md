## What is Global Score Agent?

**Global Score Agent** is the most comprehensive reputation and trust platform for the **ERC-8004** ecosystem (On-Chain Agents).

Our mission is to turn trust into a measurable, transparent, and actionable metric. We provide users, developers, investors, and protocols with a clear and objective way to evaluate the real quality of any Agent and the wallet that controls it.

In a world where anyone can create an Agent, we build the reputation layer the ecosystem needs to grow safely and sustainably.

---

## The Problem We Solve

The ERC-8004 ecosystem faces several critical challenges:

- Difficulty distinguishing legitimate, high-quality Agents from those created for speculative, sybil, or malicious purposes.
- Lack of visibility into the real reputation of the wallet that owns or registers each Agent.
- Absence of standardized, transparent on-chain trust metrics.
- High risk for users and protocols interacting with Agents without knowing their history, funding origins, or actual behavior.

Without a solid reputation layer, mass adoption of Agents is limited by distrust and risk.

## Our Mission and Objectives

We aim to become the **trust standard** of the ERC-8004 ecosystem by:

- Creating objective, auditable, and fully on-chain reputation metrics.
- Helping users make informed decisions quickly.
- Providing protocols with tools to manage risk and reward high-quality Agents.
- Fostering a healthier, more transparent, and professional ecosystem.

## Our Products

### Index HUMI (Human-like Metrics Index)
The primary reputation index for Agents. It evaluates the quality, maturity, legitimacy, and real-world behavior of any Agent in the ERC-8004 ecosystem. It is calculated daily and applies to **all** Agents — new and old, high-activity and low-activity.

### Index WAMI (Wallet Advanced Metrics Index)
The perfect complement to HUMI. It evaluates the quality, risk level, and maturity of the **wallet** that controls or registers the Agent. It analyzes funding origins, portfolio health, recent activity, and multi-chain presence.

**Together, HUMI + WAMI** deliver the most complete view of trust: both the Agent and its owner.
